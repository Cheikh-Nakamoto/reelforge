licence from the copyright holder, **before** the commercial use starts.

> **FR —** studio est libre d'usage, d'étude, de modification et de partage **à des fins non commerciales**.
> Tout usage commercial exige une licence commerciale écrite, obtenue auprès de l'auteur **avant** cet usage.
> Si vous avez un doute, considérez que c'est commercial et écrivez-nous.

## What is noncommercial (no request needed)

As the licence defines it:

- personal use for research, experiment, testing, private study, hobby projects, amateur pursuits or
  religious observance, **without any anticipated commercial application**;
- use by a charitable organisation, educational institution, public research organisation, public safety or
  health organisation, environmental protection organisation, or government institution, whatever the
  funding source.

## What is commercial (licence required)

If in doubt, assume it is commercial and ask. For example:

- editing videos with studio for a client, an employer or an agency;
- running studio inside a paid product, a SaaS, an API or a hosted service, or offering it as a feature of one;
- using studio to produce content for a channel or account that is monetised (ads, sponsorships, affiliate
  links, paid subscriptions) or that promotes a business;
- selling, renting or bundling studio, a fork of it, or its styles and templates;
- internal use inside a for-profit company.

This list explains the project's intent; the [licence text](LICENSE) is what governs.

## How to ask

1. Open an issue titled **"Commercial licence request"** on the project repository (you can keep details
   minimal there), or contact the author through their GitHub profile:
   [@cheikh-nakamoto](https://github.com/cheikh-nakamoto).
2. Say who you are, what you want to do with studio, the expected scale (videos per month, users,
   revenue model) and for how long.
3. Do not start the commercial use until you have a signed licence.

Requests are answered case by case. Small creators and non-profits that are unsure where they stand are
welcome to ask too; the goal is to know who builds a business on this work, not to block them.

## Redistribution must carry the notice

Anyone redistributing studio, modified or not, must pass on the licence text and the two
`Required Notice:` lines at the top of [LICENSE]. A fork therefore keeps pointing commercial users here.

## Third-party components

A commercial licence for studio covers studio only. Its dependencies and models keep their own licences
(see [THIRD_PARTY.md](THIRD_PARTY.md)); some carry their own conditions, for example attribution for the
Parakeet speech model (CC BY 4.0) and the GSAP "no charge" licence, which excludes no-code visual
animation builders that compete with Webflow.
