# video-intro-funk-v2 — review

Second cut of the XewXew funk intro (README script, same track), rebuilt on `motion-doctrine`, `cut-the-curve`,
`seam-craft`, `oversized-cursor` and decision 21. Generated by `storyboard.mjs` (`FMT=9x16|16x9`), rendered by
`finish.sh`. The first cut (`work/video-intro-funk`) is untouched.

## Creative calls

- **The music is the clock**, measured on the low band, not guessed: bass from 1.18 s (beat 26), drop 5.67 s (34),
  breakdown 19.14 s (58), dip 33.2–34.6 s (83–85), lift 37.10 s (90), bass hit 46.08 s (106), music cut dead on the
  end card at 47.20 s (108). v1 put the drop on beat 36; it is on 34, so "savoir." now lands on it.
- **One current, LEFT.** Every ordinary seam is a waterfall cut or cut-the-curve to the left; screen changes inside
  the phone are cut-the-curve left over 12 % of the screen. Reserved vectors, each spent once on meaning: Z push into
  the party (zoom-through), Z push into the last voice note (the emerald bubble becomes the brand's world), UP for the
  reveal (logo leaves the top as the line and the phone rise), inverse zoom for the end card (arrival).
- **Grounds tell the story**: dark for the old way (hook, voice notes), emerald for the brand, cream for the product;
  colour floods change the ground while the phone stays.
- **No idle motion.** v1's phone wobble on every beat, floating tiles and drifting blobs are gone. Between entries,
  scenes are carried by camera with intent (truck + push in the hook, dolly through depth cards, one crane on the
  voice-note wall, one orbit on "Pensé pour le téléphone"), sequenced UI life (the recordings, counters, typing) or
  staged reveals (pills, stats, chips).
- **Causal motion.** The oversized cursor (black, white stroke, one look) taps the title field on the frame the
  recording starts typing, taps "Partager sur WhatsApp" which morphs into the link a guest receives (its address types
  itself, seeded intervals), taps that link which is carried into the phone and becomes the guest's site, and presses
  the final button (press + sheen on the click frame). Fatou's answer (2 people) ticks the organiser's totals from
  8 / 17 to 9 / 19 — the same numbers the recordings show before and after.
- **The ending rhymes with the opening**: the same 3D photo row, the camera trucking one card per beat, three photos
  (README), the line dead still while the world moves behind it, "son site." on the bass hit.
- **Stillness before payoffs**: 0.3 s before the logo's gold dot, 0.4 s before the dive, 0.4 s before "savoir.".

## Warnings kept on purpose

- The phone's bottom bezel is cropped by the frame (9:16 phone at 1.67× the real screen, 16:9 at 1.31×).
- The typed address counts as 22 "words" for `line_unread` (one span per letter); it is on screen ~2.8 s.
- The legal mention holds 3.3 s (README asks ≥ 2 s; the closing-line rule would want 4.2 s, i.e. a 51.4 s film).
- In 9:16 the voice-note wall and the phone's lower half sit below the 1330 px platform line; every word to read is
  inside 168–1330 px.

## Weakest shots, and what I would change with more time

- Guests (31.5–37 s, 9:16): the pill column covers the left quarter of the phone screen. A cleaner version would
  dock the pills into a two-column mosaic above the phone on beat 86 (README asks for a mosaic at the end of the bar).
- "Tout le monde doit savoir." (4.5–6.8 s): the depth cards are small and far; bigger cards closer to the lens would
  sell the dolly more.
- The reveal opens on an empty emerald frame for half a beat while the first stroke draws.
